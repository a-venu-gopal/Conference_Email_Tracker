# Conference Email Tracker

An Excel template for tracking daily email outreach for one conference: emails sent, replies received and reply rates, with an automatic dashboard.

## What's inside

`Conference_Email_Tracker_TEMPLATE.xlsx` has three sheets:

- **Daily Tracking** – where you type. One row per day: date, SendGrid emails sent, individual emails sent, SendGrid replies, individual email replies.
- **Dashboard** – view-only. KPI cards and charts that update on their own.
- **How to Use** – short instructions, colour key and settings.

## Features

- Reply rates calculated automatically (SendGrid, individual and overall); shows 0% instead of an error when no emails were sent
- Daily and cumulative totals
- Duplicate dates are blocked, and problem rows are flagged in red
- Dashboard charts: email volume and reply split, emails sent vs replies, daily trends for the latest 30 reporting days, daily reply rate, cumulative emails and replies
- High / Medium / Low performance levels, with limits you can change

## How to use

1. Download the template and open it in Microsoft Excel.
2. On **Daily Tracking**, fill the two blue boxes: the conference name and the person handling it.
3. Add one row each day in the blue cells. Grey cells fill themselves.
4. Open **Dashboard** to see the results.
